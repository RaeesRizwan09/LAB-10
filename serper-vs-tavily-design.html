3 Feature Comparison

Serper
SERP API
  • Google results as JSON (organic, knowledge graph, People Also Ask, related searches)
  • Webpage endpoint for full content (separate, pricing after sign-in)
  • Country and language targeting
  • News, Maps, Shopping, Scholar support
  • Faster raw latency
Tavily
RAG API
  • Snippet chunks + raw page content in same request
  • Generated answer (LLM-ready synthesis)
  • RAG helpers: answer synthesis & follow-up questions
  • Mature LangChain & LlamaIndex integrations
  • 1,000 free credits / month (no card required)

Serper leaves page content to a second endpoint with no public price, and its credits expire after 6 months. Tavily costs 5–26.7× more per basic search, and advanced depth doubles that price.

4 When to Pick Serper

Tavily

    6 Decision Framework

    Use this quick guide to choose the right API for your specific needs.

    Choose Serper if:
    • Cost is your primary concern
    • You need raw Google SERP data (organic, knowledge graph, People Also Ask)
    • You need country/language targeting
    • You need News, Maps, Shopping, or Scholar support
    • You have low latency requirements
    Choose Tavily if:
    • Your LLM/RAG pipeline needs page content + synthesized answer in one request
    • You use LlamaIndex or want mature LangChain integrations
    • Your volume is small enough for 1,000 free credits / month
    • You need generated answers & follow-up question support
    • You want RAG helpers (answer synthesis)

    7 Integration Notes

    Both APIs integrate with CrewAI and LangChain ecosystems, but their integration maturity differs.

    Serper integrations: GoogleSerperAPIWrapper, CrewAI (SerperDevTool), Haystack
    Tavily integrations: Mature LangChain integration, LlamaIndex integration, CrewAI (Tavily Search Custom Tool)

    Bottom line: Pick Serper for cheap, broad Google SERP access. Pick Tavily when your agent needs to read web pages, synthesize answers, or leverage RAG pipelines. Many teams use both: Serper for initial discovery, Tavily for deep research from selected results.
